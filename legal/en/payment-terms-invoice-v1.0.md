## 1. Scope

1.1 These terms apply where the Provider has permitted the Customer to pay by
invoice.

1.2 They apply exclusively to businesses within the meaning of § 14 of the
German Civil Code (BGB).

---

## 2. Application and Approval

2.1 The Customer may request a switch from prepaid to invoicing via the Hub.

2.2 **There is no entitlement to invoice purchase.** The decision lies at the
Provider's sole discretion. A rejection requires no explanation.

2.3 Approval is provided in text form and recorded in the Hub. It may be subject
to a credit limit or further conditions.

2.4 For the review, the Customer provides the required information regarding
company name, legal form, registration, VAT identification number, address, and
invoice recipient.

2.5 **Credit check.** The Provider is entitled to obtain credit reports from
commercial credit agencies before and during the provision of invoice purchase.
The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is the mitigation
of default risk. Details are governed by the Privacy Policy at
https://blog.zarenko.net/datenschutzerklaerung-2/.

---

## 3. Revocation of Approval

3.1 The Provider may revoke approval at any time with effect for the future.
Revocation is in text form.

3.2 Revocation is particularly considered in cases of payment default,
significant deterioration of creditworthiness, incorrect information under
Section 2.4, or application for insolvency proceedings.

3.3 Upon revocation taking effect, the prepaid procedure applies again for future
orders. Invoices already issued remain due for payment.

---

## 4. Invoicing

4.1 Invoicing is carried out monthly in arrears for services rendered during the
billing period.

4.2 **Invoices are transmitted electronically.** The Customer consents to
electronic invoicing and designates a receiving address. There is no entitlement
to paper invoices.

4.3 The Customer keeps the receiving address current and ready to receive.

4.4 Objections to an invoice must be raised in text form within 30 days of
receipt. After this deadline, the invoice is deemed approved; the assertion of
legitimate objections after the deadline remains unaffected. The Customer will
be specifically informed of this effect on the invoice.

---

## 5. Payment Terms and Default

5.1 Invoices are due for payment without deduction within **14 days of receipt**.

5.2 Timely payment is determined by receipt in the Provider's account.

5.3 The Customer enters **default upon expiry of the payment period under
Section 5.1, without a reminder** (§ 286(2) no. 2 BGB); the period is calculated
by the calendar from receipt of the invoice. No separate reminder is required;
§ 286(3) BGB remains unaffected.

5.4 During default, the claim bears interest at **nine percentage points above
the base interest rate** (§ 288(2) BGB).

5.5 The Provider may claim a flat-rate default fee of EUR 40 under § 288(5)
BGB. This fee is credited against any damages owed for debt collection costs.

5.6 The assertion of further default damages remains reserved.

---

## 6. Refusal of Performance and Suspension

6.1 In the event of payment default, the Provider may withhold further services
until outstanding amounts are settled.

6.2 The Provider may suspend Hub access if the Customer is in arrears by a
non-negligible amount for more than 14 days and the suspension has been announced
in text form with at least seven days' notice.

6.3 **Suspension of Hub access does not affect Gateway operation.** Signature
processing and S/MIME signing continue. Certificate orders and support access
are affected.

6.4 Certificates already issued remain valid. However, pursuant to Section 5.5
of the Hub Terms of Use, the Provider may initiate their revocation if the
default continues for more than 60 days and has been announced with 14 days'
notice.

6.5 The Customer's payment obligation for the period of suspension remains
unaffected to the extent the suspension is attributable to the Customer.

## 7. Set-Off, Retention, Assignment

7.1 The Customer may only set off undisputed or legally established claims.

7.2 The Customer has a right of retention only to the extent their counterclaim
arises from the same contractual relationship.

7.3 The Customer may assign claims against the Provider only with the Provider's
prior consent. § 354a of the German Commercial Code (HGB) remains unaffected.

---

## 8. Credit Limit

8.1 The Provider may set a credit limit and adjust it at any time with effect
for the future.

8.2 Upon reaching the limit, the Provider may reject further orders or switch to
prepaid until outstanding amounts are settled.

8.3 The applicable limit is displayed in the Hub.

The Customer shall promptly notify the Provider in text form of:

- changes to company name, legal form, address, or invoice recipient,
- changes to the VAT identification number,
- the filing for insolvency proceedings over the Customer's assets.
